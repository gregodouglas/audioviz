# Phase 8 — the audio engine

The plan and its reasons are ADR-0006 (`docs/decisions/ADR-0006-audio-engine.md`); this is the working log, newest last.

## Progress log

- **2026-10-19, 0.4.30: step 0, hits that are never dropped.** The onset detector counts its hits per group (`OnsetDetector::count`, `FeatureFrame::onset_count`). The app compares each frame's counts with the last frame's and sets `onset_hit` from that, so `V.hits` is 1 on exactly one frame per onset. Before, it read the hop's own flag from the latest snapshot, and a hop (10.7 ms) is shorter than a frame: at 60 fps about a third of hits were lost, and at 144 fps some showed on two frames. The engine's tap-along onset log compares counts too, and the per-layer band view carries the count of the group it picks.
- **2026-10-19, 0.4.31: step 1a, capture.** Greg set the focus (capture, live history, the best predictions and signals a live stream allows) and asked for a director view (live spectrograms and aids for the visual creator, not the audience); both are now in ADR-0006. The WASAPI loop (`crates/audio/src/windows.rs`) reads every waiting packet on each wake-up (it read one, so a second could wait up to 60 ms) and sends every whole frame at once (it held back what did not fill a 256-frame chunk, up to 5 ms). While nothing plays it makes up silence at the pace of real time (it sent 256 frames per 60 ms, so the analysis clock ran at a twelfth of real time). The device's silent flag zeroes a packet; its discontinuity flag is counted and marked on the next chunk. Chunks carry a running frame clock (`frame_index`) and `discontinuity`. A full queue drops a chunk instead of stalling the device (the queue is now about 1.6 s). A failed stream (a device unplugged) reopens after 3 s instead of ending capture for good, and endpoint loopback reopens when the default output device changes. `CaptureHealth` counts real and made-up frames, glitches, late packets (40 ms or more apart while flowing), clipped samples, reopens, device changes and dropped chunks; `record.rs` prints it, and the app logs it as a second debug line each second (`capture n s real n s made up, glitches ...`). Live check on Spotify: 8 s recorded = 7.97 s real, nothing lost; the app over 12 s kept real audio level with the clock, no glitches or drops. Hand tests owed: pause Spotify (made-up silence should keep pace, `made up` growing a second a second), switch the default output while on endpoint loopback, unplug the output device.
- **2026-10-19, 0.4.32: step 1b and 1d, the history and the director view, and the WAV test bench.** The analyzer writes a `HopRecord` every hop (`crates/analysis/src/history.rs`): a 160-row log-frequency dB column (30 Hz to 16 kHz, -96 to 0 dB, rows under 300 Hz from the 8192-point transform, the rest from the 2048), the 32 bands as the visuals get them, onset strength and its threshold on one scale (`OnsetDetector::threshold`) with the hits as bits, the beat clock and whether it wrapped, bass/mid/treble, centroid, width, energy trend, silence. The engine forwards them (`Engine::drain_records`); the app keeps five minutes (`audio_view::History`, about 10 MB) and marks track changes in it. **D** opens the director view over the bottom half of the window (Esc closes it; it is in the shortcuts sheet): spectrogram, bands, onset lanes (strength bright, threshold dim, hits as ticks) for low, mid, high and all, the beat lane (phase sawtooth, confidence, the beats as a grid through every lane, the BPM), the level (RMS in dB, energy trend) and track changes; a header with capture health; hovering reads every lane at that moment (and the frequency over the spectrogram). F freezes, a drag scrolls back (and freezes; dragging back to the present follows again), the wheel zooms 2 to 120 s. It costs about 2.5 ms CPU a frame while open (1.5 to 4.0 ms on the Scope scene at 1600x900), nothing while closed. `AUDIOVIZ_WAV=<path>` plays a WAV as if live, looping, through the same engine (`crates/audio/src/wav.rs`); `AUDIOVIZ_AUDIO_VIEW=1` opens the view at start. First look, on `target/recording.wav` (a 140 BPM track): the kicks stand clear in the spectrogram, but the bass bands hang on after each one (the 171 ms window, step 2), and the low onset group fires on much that is not a kick. The beat grid sits on the kicks at 140.2 BPM. Still to do in step 1: saving the history as a session, the offline timeline sheet from `analyze_wav`, and the delay of each signal from sound to screen.
- **2026-10-20, 0.4.33: step 2, the bands.** Measured first, with `cargo run --release -p audioviz-analysis --example band_response [file.wav]` (kick trains: delay to the peak, time over half, contrast; steady tones: how many bands light; a recording: mean and flicker per band; the analysis's CPU). Before, on a 120 BPM kick sweeping 120 to 45 Hz: the bass bands peaked 86 to 103 ms after the kick and stayed over half for 193 ms; a steady 1 kHz tone lit 9 of 32 bands over 0.5, a 60 Hz tone 25 over 0.1; on a recording every band averaged 0.6 to 0.7.
  - **Filterbank** (`crates/analysis/src/filterbank.rs`): a fourth-order gammatone per band, the band turned down to 0 Hz and low-passed four times, its mean power over the hop (the end sample dipped where partials in a band beat). Bands even on ln(1 + f / 100 Hz), 30 Hz to 16 kHz (`AnalyzerConfig::band_corner_hz`): four under 140 Hz, each 20 to 35 Hz wide, against eight 6 to 28 Hz wide on the old log scale. The relative bands now peak 10 to 40 ms after a kick.
  - **What the bands say** (`crates/analysis/src/bands.rs`): the relative bands as before (each against its own best), but a band 30 dB under the loudest is no longer lifted, and they let go in 90 ms (was 200; `V.bands` keeps its own 200 ms on top); **levels**, every band on one scale over the 42 dB under the track's loud level (a peak follower, 50 ms up, 30 s down, never under -40 dB), with a 1.5 dB per octave tilt that levels typical music (measured on two recordings); **attacks**, the rise over the mean of the last three hops, full at 9 dB, fading over 80 ms (a minimum instead of the mean caught every beat dip and read 0.7 on average); **six broad bands** (sub, bass, low mid, mid, presence, air; `AnalyzerConfig::broad_edges_hz`) with level, relative to their own average, attack.
  - After, on the same kicks: attacks peak 3 to 14 ms after, relative bands 17 to 58, levels 22 to 57 (dB levels fall slowly with the sound's own decay; punch is the attacks' job). Steady tones light 3 bands' levels over 0.5 (60 Hz: 6 over 0.1, was 25). On the 140 BPM recording, attacks average 0.2 to 0.4 (a hat every sixteenth), levels 0.43 to 0.66 across the bands. The whole analysis runs at 1.1% of one core.
  - Visuals: `level(i)`, `attack(i)`, `broad(k)`, `broad_rel(k)`, `broad_attack(k)` and `SUB` .. `AIR` in the API (the uniform grew from 1200 to 1552 bytes); every factory visual still compiles. `band(i)` keeps its meaning, so visuals keep their look, but its bands sit at new frequencies (more of them in the mids, four in the bass instead of eight) and react sooner. The per-layer band view fades levels, attacks and the broad bands outside its range too. The lab's made-up groove fills them. The director view shows levels and attacks in place of the relative bands; hovering a band lane reads that band's three values, and the readout gives the broad levels.

- **2026-10-21, 0.4.35: step 3, the groove.** Measured with `cargo run --release -p audioviz-analysis --example beat_response`: made-up songs at 92, 128, 140 and 174 BPM (kick on 1 and 3, snare on 2 and 4, hats on the eighths, a bass note and a chord changing on each bar's first beat; 12 bars, a 4-bar breakdown of the chord alone, 8 bars, then a new track at another tempo), reporting lock time, tempo, phase at the true beats, stability and whether the bar's first beat is right.
  - **Before:** locks in 4.0 s; phase within 2 to 7 ms of the kicks once locked; but in the 92 BPM breakdown the tempo wandered to 128 (the chord's slightly sharp third beats at about 2 Hz) with confidence 0.91, a new track at 124 never locked (it ended on 82.7, its 2:3), and 174 is out of range (it reads 87, half time).
  - **Beat clock** (`crates/analysis/src/beat.rs`): `Analyzer::new_track(id)` (the app sends it on every track change; the engine's `Cmd::NewTrack`) forgets the tempo's evidence while the clock runs on, so nothing jumps, and starts from the tempo a track settled on before if it plays again (kept per track id for the session); the first estimate comes after 2.5 s (was 4); leaving a lock takes agreeing estimates for longer the longer it has held (1.5 s fresh, up to 6 s); a settled lock gives way only to an onset envelope with 40% of the energy that built it, and steers its phase only as hard as the envelope's energy allows (none under 30%), its energy reference rising in 2 s and falling over 30 s; a young lock on a tempo at 3:2 or 2:1 to a likelier one moves to it when three estimates agree, and to a less likely related one only on 6 s of agreement; range 70 to 185 BPM; `pulse_stability`, the share of the last 8 s of estimates that agreed.
  - **Bars** (`crates/analysis/src/bar.rs`): which of four beats is the first, from the kick and the bass's attack over each beat's first quarter and the chords changing (the unsmoothed pitch-class profile, `Chroma::raw`, over the beat's first half against the last beat's second half); four scores fading over ~8 bars, a 20% lead to take over, confidence from the lead. The smoothed profile took 0.4 s to move and could not see a chord change: kicks alone left 1 and 3 a coin toss (0% right on two songs).
  - **After:** locks in 2.5 s; every breakdown keeps its tempo and runs on with its phase steady (offsets 2 to 62 ms, spread under 0.04 beat), back on the kicks within a beat of the drums returning; new tracks lock in 2.5 s, the 124 one in 5 s (its hats on every eighth make 82.7 nearly as periodic; stability reads 0.26 there, honestly); the bar's first beat is right in every section, confidence 0.29 to 0.95. 174 BPM still reads as 87, half time: its kicks fall on every 87 BPM beat, and visuals that use the phase look right at either. On the two recordings: 17/24 and 19/24 seconds on the median tempo (was 17 and 16); the first locks when its beat comes in at 13 s, after an intro with little beat.
  - Visuals: `V.bar` (bar phase, beat in the bar, how sure of the bar, pulse stability). The director view marks the bar's first beats (a brighter line and a 1, by its confidence) and gives the tempo, how sure, how steady and the bar's confidence in its header.
- **2026-10-21, 0.4.36: step 4, context.** `crates/analysis/src/context.rs`: each hop a short description of the sound (loudness, the six broad bands, brightness, noisiness, activity, the unsmoothed pitch-class profile at half weight) averaged over 0.4, 3, 12 and 40 s, starting 2 s into a track and faded in over 8 s. The scene director's drop cue now uses the drop (it fired on an energy fall, a breakdown).
  - Measured with `cargo run --release -p audioviz-analysis --example context_response [file.wav]` on a made-up 128 BPM song (A, B with other chords and hats on sixteenths, an 8-bar build with a snare roll and a riser and no kick or bass, a drop, a 4-bar breakdown, A): build 0 through A, 0.10 in B, rising to 1.0 through the build (mean 0.51); the drop found 0.18 s after it, confidence 0.80; transitions 1.6 to 1.8 s after the build, the drop, the breakdown and the return of A; A to B (chords and hats only) not found. - **2026-10-21, 0.4.37: step 4, sections from the track's history.** `crates/analysis/src/sections.rs`: every bar leaves a summary (the context's description averaged over the bar). Sections close on a transition (snapped back 1.5 s to the bar the change began on) or on a peak of the bars' own novelty: the four bars after a bar line against the four before, known four bars later, a boundary when it peaks 1.4 times over the lowest of the last eight lines and by 0.03 (the 1.5 s novelty could not tell A from B: a chord change every bar made it as high inside a section as at its edge, 0.32 to 0.42 against 0.36 to 0.47; the four-bar comparison is low inside a section whose chords go round every two or four bars). A finished section takes the label of one it sounds like (distance under 0.085), else a new one; the current one is compared as it plays (seen before, 0..1) and, matching a finished one, is expected to last as long (the first section is partial and never sets a length), else to the next eight-bar phrase (sure 0.25).
  - Measured on a made-up song A B A B, build, drop, breakdown, A B (`context_response`, 128 BPM, B with other chords, hats on sixteenths and a bright lead): sections found at every true boundary but the end of the build (split once inside it); the second A labelled A (0.056 from the first) and the second B, B (0.058); the A after the breakdown took the drop's label (0.075 against 0.077 to A: same chords, louder); with two bars to go every eight-bar section predicted 2.0 bars, and four bars in, 4.5 (true); the four-bar breakdown was predicted as eight. - **2026-10-21, 0.4.38: step 5, links and the link lane.** Greg chose links per parameter in the panel, with the inspector lane and a test input. `crates/app/src/links.rs`: a `Link` (parameter, signal, the value at nothing and at full, response curve, rise and fall in ms, threshold, hysteresis, a confidence gate for the beat, bar, drop and phrase signals) and 32 signals in six groups (bands' levels and attacks, onsets, beat, character, context). A link is stored in the visual's parameter block on the layer (`"links": [...]`), so scene files and presets carry it (`params_json` keeps it) and switching the layer's visual away and back keeps it. Each layer's visual runs its links every frame after reading its parameters (state per link: smoothed value, on/off for the hysteresis) on the layer's band-filtered features, and the value replaces the slider's.
  - Panel: every slider of a loaded visual has a link button (three bars of a level meter, lit when linked) taken from its rail, so no row is wider (`widgets::slider_link`). Linking starts at the slider's value and runs up to the top of its range; under the slider an editor opens: Follows (grouped), At nothing, At full, Response (early / straight / late), Rise, Fall, Threshold (and Hysteresis when on), Only when sure (for inferred signals), Test input (a slider standing in for the music), a hint. Reset of the visual clears its links too.
  - The director view gets a Link lane when a link's editor has been open (`links::PROBE`, the last one focused): the signal as it came (dim), past the gate and threshold (mid), shaped and smoothed (accent), with "Gain <- Bass attack (0.60 .. 2.60) = 1.31".
